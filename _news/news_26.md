I delivered my **second Habilitation talk**, titled **"How Much Compute, How Fast, at What Cost, and for What Quality? Multidimensional Elastic Service Management across the Edge-Cloud Continuum"**, at the **University of Klagenfurt**, Austria. The talk explored multidimensional elasticity and intelligent service orchestration, addressing trade-offs among computational resources, latency, cost, and service quality across heterogeneous edge–cloud environments.
